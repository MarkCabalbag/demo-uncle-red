Our story

Reliable, honest, high-quality workmanship.

At Uncle Red’s Plumbing, we’re a locally owned and operated plumbing business committed to delivering reliable, honest and high-quality workmanship. With experience across residential plumbing, gas services and property maintenance, we take pride in practical solutions and dependable service our clients can count on.

We understand how important it is to have a plumber you can trust, especially when it comes to urgent repairs or managing rental properties. That’s why we focus on clear communication, prompt response times, and getting the job done right the first time.

As a small business, we value long-term relationships with our clients and take a hands-on approach to every job, no matter the size. All work is completed by fully licensed tradespeople and carried out to Australian Standards, with safety and attention to detail at the core of everything we do.
